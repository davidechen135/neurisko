## Interpretation (hand-written, 2026-10-30; rerunning the script overwrites this file)

- **The heuristic engine does not beat hill-climbing that has the same hall of fame.** hc+hof 0.838, engine 0.826, engine+llm 0.794. The three means differ by less than the seed-to-seed spread (every method has one seed in the 0.6s), so with 5 seeds they are tied. They are not ranked.
- **The hall of fame is what makes the difference.** Plain hill-climbing on the fixed gauntlet scores 0.629. Adding the hall of fame to the same hill-climb raises it to 0.838. The engine's gain over plain hill-climbing comes from that mechanism, not from the heuristics.
- **The LLM did not produce stronger champions** at this budget. Its heuristics were valid and useful (see the engine design notes), but they did not lead to a better final design than code-only meta-heuristics.
- **Every method converges on the same answer.** 20 of 20 champions contain witch or pumpkin_mage, and 13 contain both. The imbalance is robust to the search method, which matters more for a balance tool than which search wins.
- What the engine adds that hill-climbing does not: **readable, ranked rules** that explain *why* designs improve (e.g. "IF unspent>=1 THEN add flesh_golem at front", 40/40 improvements), and the audit trail. The balance report (Spec 3) should be built on that, not on a claim of better search.
